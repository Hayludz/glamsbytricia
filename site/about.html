The approach

Your face leads.

Every face is built differently, and a look that flatters one will fight another. So I start by reading yours — your skin, its undertone, how it behaves through the day, where the light naturally falls on you. Only then do I pick a base.

The occasion matters just as much. A church wedding in the afternoon and an owambe that runs past midnight are two different technical problems, and I solve them differently.

What stays constant is the finish: skin that still looks like skin, colour that holds, and nothing that will surprise you in your own photographs.
